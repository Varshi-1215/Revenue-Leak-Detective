# Revenue Leak Detective 🕵️‍♀️💰

An AI-powered revenue intelligence platform that discovers where businesses are losing money and identifies opportunities to recover it.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Revenue Leak Detective helps businesses identify hidden revenue losses across payments, returns, discounts, products, regions, and customer behavior.

The project analyzes business transaction data and converts the findings into actionable business insights using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Power BI.

## ❓ Business Problems Solved

- Where is the company losing money?
- Which products generate low profit?
- Which customers are at risk of leaving?
- Where are payment failures and cancellations happening?
- How much potential revenue can be recovered?
- What actions should the business take?

## 🔍 Revenue Leak Areas

- Payment failures
- Product returns
- Excessive discounts
- High-value customers at risk
- Regional revenue leakage
- Product-level revenue leakage
- Delivery-related customer dissatisfaction

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard provides:

- Total Revenue
